Physical divisions

India's major regions

1. Northern Mountains

  • Himalayas and associated ranges
  • Major source region of perennial rivers
  • Young fold mountain system

2. Northern Plains

  • Formed mainly by river deposits
  • Very fertile agricultural region
  • Includes Indus–Ganga–Brahmaputra plains

3. Peninsular Plateau

  • Ancient stable landmass
  • Deccan Plateau and Central Highlands
  • Rich in many mineral resources

4. Coastal Plains & Islands

  • Western and Eastern Coastal Plains
  • Arabian Sea and Bay of Bengal coasts
  • Andaman & Nicobar and Lakshadweep islands
Quick revision

Fast facts

India's location: India lies in South Asia and is surrounded by the Himalayas in the north and the Indian Ocean to the south.

Highest peak in India: Kangchenjunga (8,586 m), on the India–Nepal border region.

Major river system: The Ganga river system is one of the most important drainage systems of northern India.

Monsoon: The southwest monsoon is the principal rainy season for most of India.

Three sides of water: Arabian Sea — west; Bay of Bengal — east; Indian Ocean — south.
